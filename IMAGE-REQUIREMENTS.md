# PRO1ST — Image Requirements

What the site needs from you, in priority order.

**Where to upload:** Shopify admin → **Content → Files**. Product photography
goes on the product itself (Products → *product* → Media). Anything uploaded to
Shopify is picked up without a code deploy; only the two items marked
`needs one-line change` require me to point a section at the new file.

**Specs for everything below**
| | |
|---|---|
| Format | JPEG or WebP (WebP preferred) |
| Long edge | 2400px minimum, 3000px ideal |
| File size | Under 2MB each after export |
| Colour | sRGB |
| Naming | `pro1st-<subject>-<01>.jpg`, lowercase, hyphens |

Shoot **horizontal and vertical versions** of anything marked ⇄ — the gallery
wall uses both orientations and currently has to crop.

---

## What you already have

25 products × ~5 photographs = **125 images**, all live on the site. The
product photography is genuinely good and is not the problem.

What's missing is **everything that isn't a product on a white background**.
That is why the site reads as a catalogue rather than a brand.

---

## Priority 1 — unlocks sections that are currently improvising

### 1.1 Workshop / assembly floor ⇄ · **5–8 shots** · *highest impact*
The Origin and Craft pages are written about a facility nobody can see. Right
now Origin falls back to a product photo, which is why the brand story feels
thin.

- Wide shot of the assembly floor
- Benches with chassis mid-assembly
- Hands working — biasing output pairs, winding, soldering
- Components in trays / stock shelving
- Bench testing or burn-in racks
- The Moti Cinema Compound frontage or unit entrance

*Unlocks:* Origin hero image, Craft panels, About storytelling.
**Needs one-line change** to point Origin at the real photo.

### 1.2 Products in real use ⇄ · **6–10 shots**
The single biggest driver of "premium" perception. A speaker on white is a
product; a speaker at a wedding is a reason to buy.

- Live event / DJ setup with the rig visible
- Wedding or function with speakers on stands
- An installed system in a venue, hall, or place of worship
- Someone operating a mixer mid-event
- Microphone in use on stage
- Trolley speaker being wheeled / loaded

*Unlocks:* hero background option, gallery wall, category headers.
Available light is fine — these should look real, not staged.

### 1.3 Showroom, interior and exterior · **3–4 shots**
- Storefront with signage
- Interior with product on display
- Counter / service desk

*Unlocks:* the contact section beside the map, which is currently map-only.

## Priority 2 — makes the catalogue sell harder

### 2.1 Scale references · **4–6 shots**
A 12″ trolley speaker means nothing floating on white. One person in frame
fixes it. One per major product family: speaker, amplifier, mixer, microphone.

### 2.2 Full-range group shot ⇄ · **2–3 shots**
The whole line together, dark background, dramatic side light. Prime hero and
Arsenal material.

### 2.3 Packing and dispatch · **3–4 shots**
Boxes, labelling, tape, the loading bay, stacked cartons. Supports the dealer
and bulk-order story, which is where the margin is.

### 2.4 Detail macros ⇄ · **6–8 shots**
Terminals, grille mesh, heat-sink fins, knobs, cable strain relief, port
labelling. The gallery wall is built for exactly this.

---

## Priority 3 — brand depth

### 3.1 Team at work · **2–4 shots**
Candid, not posed portraits. Faces build trust in a trade business.

### 3.2 Logo variants — *not photography, but blocking a design compromise*
Your logo is multi-colour artwork for a light background. The navy **P** sits
at about **1.7:1** contrast on the dark UI, which is unreadable, so the header
currently places the logo on a white plate.

Supply either and the plate can go:
- **Single-colour white/knockout** version (SVG preferred)
- **Single-colour black** version for light theme

### 3.3 Open Graph / social card · **1 image, 1200×630**
Currently generated from type. A real photograph would perform better when the
site is shared on WhatsApp — which, in this trade, is where it will be shared.

## Product data, while you're in the admin

|---|---|

| Vendor inconsistent | "Desire Electronics" vs "Professional Rhythm Operator - PRO 1st" |
| No `custom.featured` flags | Homepage featuring falls back to a hardcoded list |

the missing specifications — 17 pages of it.
